Projects and videos

2026 to present · MSc project

Autonomous colour-based pick-and-place

A Leo Rover finds a coloured object, drives to it, picks it up and places it in the bin of the same colour.

An Intel RealSense depth camera gives colour detection and 3D position. Nav2 handles navigation and obstacle avoidance. Everything is tested in Gazebo before it runs on the real rover.

  • ROS 2
  • Nav2
  • Gazebo
  • Intel RealSense
  • Leo Rover

Externally influenced speed control

An Arduino-based ECU with Wi-Fi modules that limits an electric vehicle’s speed inside set zones. It improved safety and compliance by 40% and cut response time by 20%. Published at ICIRIAC.
